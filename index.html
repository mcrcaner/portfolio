About me.

I'm a graphic designer based in Izmir. I make pop-up books and cards, print and editorial projects, and digital interfaces. I'm just as happy folding paper as I am working out a UX/UI flow.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably playing Tetris. There's a board on the Play page if you want to try.

\\ HOW A POP-UP GETS MADE

A pop-up starts as a rough paper model. I fold and cut until the mechanism works. Then I redraw the parts on the computer, design the artwork on them, print everything and put it together by hand.

\\ ABOUT[ ESC ] CLOSE ✕

Made by hand, then refined.

I'm a graphic designer based in Izmir. I design pop-up books and cards, print and editorial projects, and digital interfaces, and I'm just as happy folding paper as I am structuring a UX/UI flow.


What connects it all is clarity. A pop-up should open with one pull, a page should be easy to read, and a screen shouldn't need instructions.

\\ HOW I WORK

01 Build the mechanism in plain paper until it works.
02 Redraw each part digitally and design the graphics on it.
03 Print the parts, cut them out and assemble the piece by hand.

My UX/UI work follows the same order: structure first, visual design on top.
